# GSTFY Product Definition

This folder defines GSTFY as a product, not only as a collection of backend and frontend modules.

GSTFY is a GST-first business operations SaaS for Indian small and micro businesses. The product assumption is simple:

Small dealers do not need a heavy enterprise accounting system. They need a guided way to create bills, track purchases, manage stock, collect payments, and prepare GST returns without depending on a Chartered Accountant for routine work.

## Core Product Thesis

Tally Prime, Zoho Books, and Vyapar are useful tools, but they often feel too broad, too accounting-heavy, or too operationally complex for small shop owners. GSTFY focuses on the daily flow of a dealer:

```text
Sell goods
Record purchases
Track stock
Collect or pay money
Review GST
File with confidence
```

GSTFY should feel like a GST assistant for the business owner, not like an accounting textbook.

## Who This Folder Is For

- Product planning and feature prioritization.
- Engineering alignment before building new modules.
- Customer discovery and pitch refinement.
- Explaining why GSTFY exists and how it differs from larger accounting tools.
- Keeping business, UX, and backend decisions connected to the same product thesis.

## Research Methods Used

- Affinity mapping to group repeated dealer and CA pain points.
- Empathy mapping to define what small dealers and CA partners say, think, do, and feel.
- SWOT analysis for the requested SOWRT-style review of GSTFY against Tally Prime, Zoho Books, and Vyapar.

## Important Positioning

GSTFY is not trying to become a full enterprise ERP in the first phase.

GSTFY is trying to win the smallest GST-registered business by making the essential compliance workflow faster, safer, and easier than paying a CA every month for routine filing.
