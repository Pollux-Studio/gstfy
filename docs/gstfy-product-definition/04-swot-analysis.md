# 04 SWOT Analysis

This SWOT analysis compares GSTFY's product position against broad accounting tools such as Tally Prime, Zoho Books, and Vyapar.

## Strengths

- GST-first product focus for Indian small businesses.
- Dealer-friendly workflows instead of accountant-first workflows.
- Tenant and subdomain model for each business.
- CA and self-service paths in the same product.
- Strong engine separation: Tax, Accounting, Inventory, Party, Product, Payment, Returns, ITC, Filing.
- Historical snapshots protect old invoices from later master-data edits.
- Users and roles allow owners to control cashier, manager, and accountant access.
- POS and daily billing are connected to GST and accounting output.
- CA referral model can create a distribution channel.

## Weaknesses

- New brand compared with Tally, Zoho, and Vyapar.
- GSTN, e-invoice, and e-way bill integrations require production provider access.
- Small dealers may still need onboarding support.
- More compliance responsibility means stronger testing and audit requirements.
- Support load may be high because GST questions are domain-heavy.
- Some advanced features are still future work, such as WhatsApp delivery, AI review, subscriptions, and e-way bill.

## Opportunities

- Many micro and small businesses pay CAs only for routine GST filing.
- Dealers want tools that save money and time, not just more software.
- CAs can onboard clients through referral codes and use GSTFY as a managed client workspace.
- Small businesses increasingly accept cloud tools and digital payments.
- GST compliance, ITC reconciliation, e-invoice, and e-way bill are recurring pain points.
- Vertical focus can beat broad accounting tools for the smallest GST user.

## Threats

- Tally, Zoho, and Vyapar can simplify their own GST flows.
- Free billing apps can compete on price.
- GST portal rule changes can force urgent product updates.
- Government or GSP API access changes can affect filing and verification features.
- Trust barrier is high for financial and compliance software.
- If GSTFY becomes too feature-heavy, it can repeat the complexity problem it is trying to solve.

## Strategic Conclusion

GSTFY should not compete by becoming a bigger accounting tool.

GSTFY should compete by being the fastest and clearest path from daily business activity to GST-ready filing output.

```text
Tally / Zoho / Vyapar:
Accounting and business software with GST features.

GSTFY:
GST compliance assistant with business operations built around it.
```
