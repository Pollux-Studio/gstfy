# 02 Target Users And Empathy Map

## Primary Users

| User | Description | Main Need |
|---|---|---|
| Kirana or retail shop owner | Small GST-registered shop with daily counter sales | Fast billing and GST summary |
| Small trader or distributor | Buys and sells goods, often B2B | Purchase, sales, ITC, and outstanding tracking |
| Freelancer or consultant | Service provider with fewer invoices | Simple GST invoices and return summary |
| Small manufacturer | Handles raw materials, stock, sales, and purchases | Inventory plus GST compliance |
| CA or accountant | Manages many small businesses | Client data, filing readiness, and review |
| Cashier or staff user | Handles daily billing at a branch | Limited POS and sales access |

## Empathy Map: Small Dealer

### Says

- "I only want to make the bill correctly."
- "My CA asks for sales and purchase details every month."
- "I do not know if GST should be CGST/SGST or IGST."
- "I do not want to touch too many settings."
- "I need to know how much tax I have to pay."

### Thinks

- "If I make a mistake, the GST portal or CA will create trouble later."
- "Accounting software is for accountants."
- "I do not know which report is important."
- "If the software is hard, I will go back to manual or WhatsApp bills."

### Does

- Creates bills from memory or a simple billing app.
- Sends purchase bills to CA over WhatsApp.
- Tracks payments manually or in notebooks.
- Checks stock only when an item is missing.
- Calls CA before filing.

### Feels

- Worried about GST mistakes.
- Irritated by complex accounting terms.
- Dependent on CA even for small routine filing.
- Comfortable with simple mobile-like flows.
- Confident when the software explains the next step.

### Pains

- GST filing fear.
- Manual data sharing with CA.
- Complex tools.
- No clear view of receivables and payables.
- Product GST rate confusion.
- Stock mismatch.
- Lack of trust in self-filing.

### Gains

- Save monthly CA fee for simple compliance.
- File GST faster.
- Know tax payable before due date.
- Avoid manual purchase and sales collection.
- Build confidence through review checks.
- Keep business data organized.

## Empathy Map: CA Partner

### Says

- "Clients send incomplete data."
- "I spend too much time asking for bills."
- "Small clients cannot use heavy software properly."
- "I need a simple dashboard for all clients."

### Thinks

- "If the client enters clean data, filing is much faster."
- "I need to know which clients are ready and which have issues."
- "I should not manually chase every client each month."

### Does

- Creates client list.
- Shares referral code or invite link.
- Reviews client sales, purchases, ITC, and GST reports.
- Follows up on missing purchase bills or mismatches.

### Feels

- Overloaded during filing dates.
- Frustrated with messy client data.
- Interested in tools that reduce repetitive work.

### Pains

- Manual data collection.
- Low client discipline.
- Duplicate work across clients.
- No simple client readiness view.

### Gains

- Faster filing review.
- Better client data.
- Scalable client management.
- Referral-based onboarding.
- Clear audit and reporting trail.
